Tactical Growth Framework

The Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Audit Checklist

A step-by-step diagnostic framework with tactical UI examples to uncover conversion bottlenecks, streamline checkout funnels, and maximize revenue per visitor.

Pillar 1: Value Proposition & Above-the-Fold Messaging

  • ✓
    The 5-Second Clarity Test: Can a first-time visitor understand what product or service is offered, who it is for, and what primary benefit it delivers within 5 seconds without scrolling?
  • ✓
    Primary CTA Contrast: Does the primary call-to-action button utilize a high-contrast accent color that stands out against background imagery and secondary nav links?
  • ✓
    Singular Action Hierarchy: Avoid offering 4 competing options above the fold. Guide the user toward one primary action (e.g. "Start Free Trial" or "Book Discovery Call").
  • ✓
    Risk Reversal Micro-copy: Feature reassuring micro-copy directly under action buttons (e.g. "No credit card required", "Cancel anytime", "Free 14-day trial").

Pillar 2: Form Ergonomics & Lead Capture

  • ✓
    Field Count Minimization: Reduce inquiry form fields to the absolute minimum. Cutting fields from 6 down to 3 typically lifts lead completion rates by over 25%.
  • ✓
    Inline Real-Time Validation: Validate inputs as the user types rather than displaying a massive list of errors upon clicking submit.
  • ✓
    Appropriate Mobile Keyboards: Ensure email inputs trigger the email keyboard (type="email") and phone fields trigger the dial-pad (type="tel").

Pillar 3: Trust Architecture & Social Proof

  • ✓
    Contextual Review Placement: Place customer quotes and star ratings adjacent to the checkout button or form rather than burying them on a distant sub-page.
  • ✓
    Verified Client Case Metrics: Highlight tangible numbers (e.g. "+140% pipeline velocity", "$4,500 retainer closed") instead of vague generic testimonials.
  • ✓
    Recognized Security Badges: Display SSL, Stripe verified billing, or relevant industry certifications near payment entry forms.

Pillar 4: Mobile Viewport & Thumb-Zone Ergonomics

  • ✓
    Sticky Mobile Bottom Bar: For mobile users, maintain a sticky bottom CTA bar so the booking button is always within comfortable one-handed thumb reach.
  • ✓
    Ample Tap Spacing: Ensure at least 8px of padding between clickable links or icons to prevent frustrating mis-taps and accidental navigation.
