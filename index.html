AuditPilot Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 22, 2026 • Compliant with EU GDPR, UK GDPR, and California CCPA/CPRA

At AuditPilot Inc. ("AuditPilot", "we", "us", or "our"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy informs you about how we collect, process, store, and safeguard your data when you visit our website, use our automated audit engine, or subscribe to our SaaS plans.

1. Data Controller

AuditPilot Inc. is the data controller responsible for personal data collected through the AuditPilot platform. If you have any inquiries regarding data processing, our Data Protection Officer (DPO) may be contacted at privacy@auditpilot.us.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, work email address, hashed authentication credentials, and agency details when creating an account.
  • Audited Domain Data: Public website URLs submitted to our scanner, including public HTTP response headers, SSL certificate expiry, publicly accessible sitemap files, and page load telemetry. We do not scan or store private backend databases or non-public administrative pages.
  • Outreach & CRM Data: Client prospect names, estimated deal values, and notes entered into your private outreach pipeline.
  • Payment & Billing Data: Payment details are tokenized and processed exclusively by Stripe Inc. (PCI-DSS Level 1 certified). AuditPilot never receives or stores your full credit card number or CVV code.
  • Technical Usage Data: IP address, browser user-agent, session identifiers, and aggregated feature interactions to maintain security and enforce rate limits.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Article 6)

We process your personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Performance of a Contract: To provision your AuditPilot workspace, calculate performance loss estimates, and process subscriptions.
  • Legitimate Interests: To detect abuse, prevent automated denial-of-service scans, and improve scanning algorithms.
  • Consent: For non-essential analytics cookies and marketing communications, which can be modified at any time.

4. Third-Party Subprocessors

We work with established enterprise subprocessors adhering to stringent data protection standards:

  • Stripe, Inc. — Payment gateway and Customer Portal management.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — Content delivery network, SSL termination, and DDoS mitigation.

5. Data Retention & Security

Your account data is retained for the active duration of your subscription. We implement 256-bit AES encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS 1.3) and at rest. If you choose to close your account, all associated saved audits and pipeline data are permanently purged within 30 days.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR & CCPA

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Data Portability: Instantly download all your audits and pipeline leads in structured JSON format via our Privacy Settings modal.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): Delete your account and purge all stored data with one click.
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate profile or agency information.
  • Right to Restrict or Object: Opt-out of non-essential analytics and tracking.

7. Contact & Regulatory Recourse

To exercise your privacy rights, please click "Privacy Settings" in your dashboard or email privacy@auditpilot.us. You also hold the right to lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority (e.g. ICO in the UK, CNIL in France, or California Privacy Protection Agency).
