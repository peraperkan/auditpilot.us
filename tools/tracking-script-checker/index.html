Common Tracking Disasters AuditPilot Catches

1. Double-Firing Conversion Tags

Firing a Purchase event without revenue or currency parameters leaves ad algorithms blind to ROAS. AuditPilot checks payload structure to ensure ad platforms receive accurate revenue numbers.
