Comprehensive On-Page SEO Inspection in 1 Click

Most SEO extensions display cluttered tables of raw data. AuditPilot structures technical findings into client-ready diagnostic insights with clear severity ratings.

Heading Hierarchy (H1-H6)

Instantly map the semantic structure of any page. Detect missing H1 tags, duplicate primary headings, and skipped levels (e.g. H2 to H4) that confuse search crawlers.

Meta Title & Description Inspector

Verify pixel width, character count truncation warnings, and keyword presence. Ensure meta descriptions maximize click-through rate (CTR) on Google SERPs.

Canonical & Indexability Signals

Inspect rel="canonical" tags, meta robots directives (noindex/nofollow), X-Robots headers, and hreflang localization attributes to prevent duplicate content penalties.

Image Alt Text & Asset Audit

Audit all images on the page for missing alt attributes, excessive file weights, and missing width/height attributes that cause Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS).

OpenGraph & Twitter Cards

Preview social share previews across LinkedIn, X/Twitter, and Facebook. Spot broken og:image links and missing og:title tags before sharing content.

JSON-LD Structured Data

Validate Schema.org microdata (Organization, LocalBusiness, FAQPage, Article) for syntax errors and missing required entity attributes.
